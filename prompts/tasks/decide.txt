Produce a structured decision brief for the following situation.

Situation: {situation}

Decision Brief Format:
1. Decision Title (one line)
2. Context (2-3 sentences)
3. Options (2-3 options, each with pros and cons)
4. Recommended Option + Rationale
5. Risk Level: Low / Medium / High + explanation
6. Reversibility: Reversible / Partially reversible / Irreversible
7. Expected Upside
8. Expected Downside
9. What happens if delayed?

Be precise and executive. No filler.
